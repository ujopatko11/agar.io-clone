    Gameplay

    • Move your mouse on the screen to move your character.
    • Press W or tap the feed button to eject mass. Feed a virus 7 times to split it in the direction of the shots.
    • Press Space or tap the split button to split your cells.
    • Eat food and other players in order to grow your character (food respawns every time a player eats it).
    • A player's mass is the number of food particles eaten.
    • Objective: Try to get fat and eat other players.
